CoreChat

High-Performance P2P Moderated Messaging

Serverless, encrypted, and moderated by CensorCore.

Overview

CoreChat is a production-ready communication platform that utilizes WebRTC to establish direct connections between users. Unlike traditional chat apps, CoreChat does not store your messages on a server. Everything happens directly in your browser.

Key Features

  • Peer-to-Peer Architecture: No central database. Messages are sent directly from one device to another.
  • Automated Moderation: Real-time filtering via the CensorCore engine ensures a safe environment.
  • Zero Footprint: No accounts, no tracking, and no persistent logs.
  • End-to-End Security: Uses DTLS/SRTP encryption standards native to WebRTC.

How it Works

CoreChat operates as a Static Web Application (SWA) with a decentralized signaling model.

The Connection Lifecycle

  1. Signaling: You receive a unique 6-character ID. When you share this with a peer, a "handshake" occurs via a signaling server.
  2. NAT Traversal: STUN servers identify your public IP to bypass home firewalls.
  3. DataChannel: A direct tunnel is opened. Once connected, the signaling server is no longer involved.

Safety Standards

To prevent abuse in a serverless environment, CoreChat implements:

  • Fuzzy-Logic Reporting: Detects "leetspeak" variations of blocked words.
  • Progressive Suspension: Automatic 1–3 minute messaging blocks for repeat violators.
  • Client-Side Scans: Outgoing messages are scanned before they leave your device.
